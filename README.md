# HPC Opportunities

Supercomputing club at Georgia Tech has compiled a list of organizations that offer internships, fellowships, and experiences in HPC and applications of HPC. This is a working list, so check back for new entries. Some entries may be more related to HPC, and some less, but in general these programs will involve or support scientific research.

We are based in the US (Atlanta, Georgia specifically), so many of these internships will be based in the US, but slowly but surely, international entries will be added.

The goal of this list is to ensure someone who wants to intern in the HPC space is aware of what's out there, so links will expire as time passes. Dead entries are left to show a snippet of what this organization offers (without needing to click into every site) as they're likely to provide internships of this nature year after year.

**Acronyms**
- LPR - Legal Permanent Resident

If you find any other internships from outside the sheet, feel free to make a pull request or let us know at supercomputing.gt@gmail.com.

## 📝 Applying to HPC Internships via Zintellect

[Zintellect](https://www.zintellect.com) is the application portal for many **high-performance computing (HPC)** research opportunities sponsored by the Department of Defense (DoD) and Department of Energy (DOE) through **ORISE**.

- Central hub for programs like:
  - **DoD HPCMP HIP** (High Performance Computing Internship Program)
  - **SULI** (DOE Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships)
  - Other HPC-related research appointments across national labs
- For **DoD HPCMP**, Zintellect links directly with the [HPCMP Project Catalog](https://orise.orau.gov/hpcmp/project-catalog.html):
  - Each listing provides a **reference code, host lab, and project scope** (e.g., HPC algorithms, scalable systems, AI/ML on supercomputers).
  - Applicants can select up to **5 preferred projects** when submitting their application.
- Why it matters: If you want to work on **world-class supercomputers** at labs like AFRL, ERDC, or Navy DoD centers, Zintellect is the gateway to apply.

## 🔍 How to Keep This List Current

Deadlines shift every cycle, so here's where to look when refreshing this repo (or hunting on your own):

- **[ACM SIGHPC Education Chapter's Undergraduate Opportunities list](https://sighpceducation.acm.org/resources/ugopportunities)** — community-maintained, HPC-specific, and lists exact dates/deadlines each cycle. Best single cross-check source.
- **[Zintellect / ORISE Opportunity Catalog](https://www.zintellect.com/Catalog)** — search "HPC," "high performance computing," or a specific lab; DoD/DOE postings often appear here first.
- **[DOE SULI/CCI program page](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli)** — SULI runs **three cycles a year (Fall, Spring, Summer)**, so check back three times annually instead of once. Deadlines run roughly: Summer ≈ early Jan, Fall ≈ late May, Spring ≈ Oct 1 (confirm exact dates each year).
- **Individual lab education/careers pages** — postings often show up here before hitting aggregator sites: ORNL ([education.ornl.gov](https://education.ornl.gov/undergraduate/)), LANL ([lanl.gov/engage/collaboration/student-programs](https://www.lanl.gov/engage/collaboration/student-programs)), Sandia ([sandia.gov/careers](https://www.sandia.gov/careers/careers/students-and-postdocs/internships-co-ops/)), LLNL ([computing.llnl.gov/careers](https://computing.llnl.gov/careers/interns)), PNNL ([careers.pnnl.gov](https://careers.pnnl.gov/)), NREL, ANL.
- **Chip/HPC vendor "Students" career pages** — these list intern *and* co-op reqs, usually with the term spelled out right in the job title (e.g., "2027 Undergrad Software Engineer Intern/Co-op"): [AMD](https://careers.amd.com/students/jobs), [Intel](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000021775/programs/jobs-at-intel.html), [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/careers/university/).
- **Community tech-internship trackers** — general (not HPC-specific), but useful for catching Fall/Spring/co-op postings across hundreds of companies as they drop: [SimplifyJobs/Pitt CSC "Off-Season" list](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships/blob/dev/README-Off-Season.md) and similar repos (e.g., vanshb03/SummerXXXX-Internships). Filter by keywords like "HPC," "compute," "silicon," "hardware," or "systems."
- **[NASA STEM Gateway](https://stemgateway.nasa.gov)** for OSTEM (3 sessions/year: Spring, Summer, Fall) and **[USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov)** (search "Pathways") for NASA's rotational co-op program.
- **[HPCwire — Off the Wire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/)** — frequently reports when labs open new student-program cohorts.

We refresh the sections below using these sources on a rolling basis — PRs welcome if you spot something newer.

## ☀️ Summer Internships

Traditional 10–16 week programs running roughly May–August. Where possible these point at the actual open requisition rather than just the department homepage — check the posting for the live status before applying, since individual reqs close on their own schedule.

- **[Summer 2027]** **[LLNL — Computing Intern (HPC / Cyber / Data Science / AI-ML)](https://computing.llnl.gov/careers/interns)** — Livermore, CA. Application window currently open **Sep 1, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027**; undergrad and grad roles across exascale performance, parallel/distributed systems, and scientific visualization.
- **[Summer 2027]** **[PNNL — Undergraduate Intern, High Performance Computing](https://careers.pnnl.gov/jobs/5931?lang=en-us)** — Richland, WA. Direct posting from PNNL's HPC group: performance modeling, compilers, runtime systems, non-von Neumann computing.
- **[Summer 2027 — Rolling]** **[NVIDIA — Deep Learning and HPC Performance Engineering Intern](https://prosple.com/graduate-employers/nvidia/jobs-internships/deep-learning-and-hpc-performance-engineering-intern)** — GPU performance benchmarking across HPC/DL frameworks; NVIDIA reviews applications on a rolling basis, so apply early.
- **[Summer 2027]** **[AMD — Student Jobs board](https://careers.amd.com/students/jobs)** — filter for the current grad-year summer reqs (titles are dated, e.g. "2027 Undergrad Software Engineer Intern").
- **[Summer 2027]** **[Sandia — Summer Internship listings](https://www.sandia.gov/careers/students/internships/)** — 10–12 weeks, May through late August; filter the careers portal by Job Family = Student.
- **[Summer 2027]** **[DoD HPCMP Project Catalog (HIP) via Zintellect](https://orise.orau.gov/hpcmp/project-catalog.html)** — undergrad/grad/postdoc HPC research placements at DoD supercomputing centers (AFRL, ERDC, Navy).
- **[Summer 2027]** **[SULI Summer Term (DOE)](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli)** — 10 weeks across 17 DOE national labs; Summer 2026 applications were due Jan 7, 2026 — expect a similar January deadline each year.

## 🔁 Co-op / Cooperative Education Programs

Longer, full-time placements (typically one academic term or more) that usually postpone graduation by a semester in exchange for deeper project ownership.

- **[Co-op 2027]** **[AMD — 2027 Undergrad Software Engineer Intern/Co-op](https://careers.amd.com/students/jobs/90947?lang=en-us)** — multiple locations; explicitly dual-listed as Intern/Co-op, so you can indicate preference during the process.
- **[Co-op 2027]** **[AMD — 2027 Undergrad Compiler Engineering Intern/Co-op](https://careers.amd.com/students/jobs/91865?lang=en-us)** — multiple locations; compiler/toolchain work for AMD hardware.
- **[Sandia Co-Op Program](https://www.sandia.gov/careers/careers/students-and-postdocs/internships-co-ops/)** — 3–8 months during an academic term (can extend into the adjacent summer); can be rotational across technical areas. Requires a formal co-op agreement between your university, Sandia, and you.
- **[NASA Pathways Intern Employment Program](https://www.usajobs.gov)** (search "Pathways" on USAJOBS) — NASA's formal work-study co-op; alternates school terms with paid, full-time civil-servant work tours and can convert directly to a full-time offer. U.S. citizens only. Postings open in narrow windows, so check often or set a USAJOBS alert.
- **[LANL — Undergraduate Student Internship Program (year-round)](https://www.lanl.gov/careers/career-options/student-internships/undergraduate/index.php)** — can be structured as an extended, co-op-style placement alongside summer research.
- **NVIDIA 8–12 month internships** (e.g., Developer Technology Engineering — HPC & AI, GPU architecture) — functionally co-op length even though NVIDIA doesn't always use the word "co-op"; search current reqs on the [university careers page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/careers/university/).
- **[Intel — Jobs at Intel: student internship & co-op program](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000021775/programs/jobs-at-intel.html)** — longer-term placement during an academic semester, distinct from the summer-only internship track.

## 🍂 Fall & Spring (Off-Cycle) Internships

Programs and postings specifically for the fall or spring semester, not just leftover summer roles.

- **[Tesla — Internship, Software Engineer, AI Data Infrastructure (Fall 2026)](https://www.tesla.com/careers/search/job/internship-software-engineer-ai-data-infrastructure-fall-2026-269829)** — Palo Alto, CA. Fleet-scale telemetry/data-infra pipeline work (HPC-adjacent, high-throughput distributed systems); starts Aug/Sep 2026, runs through Dec 2026 with possible extension into Winter/Spring 2027. A good example of the kind of listing to search for directly on [tesla.com/careers](https://www.tesla.com/careers) — Tesla, AMD, and NVIDIA all name the term directly in the job title.
- **[SULI — Fall Term (DOE)](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli)** — 16 weeks, roughly late August–December; the Fall 2026 deadline was May 20, 2026 — expect a similar spring deadline for Fall 2027.
- **[SULI — Spring Term (DOE)](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli/How-to-Apply)** — 16 weeks, roughly January–May; Spring 2027 deadline is September 30, 2026.
- **[NASA OSTEM](https://stemgateway.nasa.gov)** — 3 sessions per year (Spring, Summer, Fall), each with its own deadline; Spring 2027 session deadline is September 14, 2026.
- **[LLNL — Computing Intern](https://computing.llnl.gov/careers/interns)** — the Sep 1, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027 application window (listed under Summer above) also covers Fall/Spring-start placements; specify your availability in the application.
- Also check **[AMD](https://careers.amd.com/students/jobs)**, **[NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/careers/university/)**, and **[Intel](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000021775/programs/jobs-at-intel.html)**'s student pages directly for reqs titled "Fall 2026" or "Winter/Spring 2027."

## 📌 Tips

- Most **DOE/DoD programs** (e.g., SULI, NASA, DoD HIP) require **U.S. citizenship**. Some allow permanent residents.
- Many summer apps open **October–December**; SULI Fall/Spring apps open on their own separate cycles (see above) — prepare your resume, transcript, and references ahead of each window.
- Highlight experience with **Linux, Python/C/C++, MPI/OpenMP, CUDA, or cluster management** in applications.
- For rolling internships (LANL, PNNL, Argonne), apply **as early as possible** for priority.
- **Co-ops require planning with your academic advisor** — most require stepping away from coursework for a full term, so confirm how it affects your graduation timeline before committing.
